Jal Drishti resolves the bottleneck of flash flood unpredictability through a deterministic multi-signal decision engine. It fuses empirical machine learning predictions with real-time satellite data and hydrological physics.

The Challenge

Monsoonal flash floods in the Himalayan terrain develop within 15-60 minutes, rendering conventional single-source gauge networks or delayed satellite passes insufficient.

Unreliable cellular connectivity in valleys required a robust outbound alert mechanism that bypassed standard internet drops.

The Solution

We built a dual-engine architecture: integrating a Live REST/MQTT pipeline with an XGBoost Champion Classifier. Features include Copernicus 30m DEM slope gradients and GPM IMERG satellite precipitation tracking.

To alert citizens reliably, we incorporated automated outbound voice call dispatch via Twilio (TwiML / Edge-TTS) directly to standard mobile phones.
